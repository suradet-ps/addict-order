# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a safe, respectful place for pharmacists,
developers, designers, and hospital staff to collaborate. We commit to
respectful participation regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Focus on the problem, not the person.
- Give credit for ideas and work.
- Respect patient privacy and hospital confidentiality at all times.
- Follow the security and privacy rules in `SECURITY.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing patient data, credentials, or confidential hospital information.
- Deliberately introducing a security or privacy defect.
- Ignoring the read-only boundary of HOSxP.
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional hospital setting.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat, and events. It also applies when representing the project in
public.

## Reporting

Report a conduct issue to the project maintainers privately. Do not use a
public issue for a conduct report. The maintainers will review the report and
respond as quickly as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code.
Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from
the project.

## Attribution

This code is a short project-specific policy. It is not a legal document and
does not replace hospital policy or applicable law.
